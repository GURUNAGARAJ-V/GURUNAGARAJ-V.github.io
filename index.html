The Cost of Poor Planning

When traditional infrastructure scales without ecological foresight, the damage is catastrophic. This is what we must engineer against.

🏭

Urban Heat Islands

Excessive concrete and asphalt trap heat, raising city temperatures by up to 5°F and drastically increasing energy consumption.

🌊

Wastewater Runoff

Poor drainage planning causes untreated stormwater to flood local ecosystems, destroying aquatic life and contaminating drinking supplies.

🗑️

Material Waste

The construction sector contributes to 30% of global waste. Without BIM and smart planning, millions of tons of material rot in landfills.
